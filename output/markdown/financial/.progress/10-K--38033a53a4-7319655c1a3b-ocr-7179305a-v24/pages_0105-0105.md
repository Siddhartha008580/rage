The impairment test performed for intangible assets as of July 31, 2025 and 2023 indicated no impairment charges were required. 

The impairment tests performed for intangible assets as of July 31, 2024 indicated that an impairment charge was required for indefinitelived intangible assets related to certain acquired open-end management contracts, primarily driven by quantitative factors, such as reduced growth expectations, a decrease in revenue basis points and net client outflows. As a result, the Company recorded a noncash impairment charge of $50 million, which is included within amortization and impairment of intangible assets expense on the consolidated statements of income for the year ended December 31, 2024. No impairment charges were required for any other intangible assets.
