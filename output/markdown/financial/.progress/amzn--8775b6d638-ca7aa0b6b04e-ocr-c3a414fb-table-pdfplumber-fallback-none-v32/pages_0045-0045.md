


# **_Uncertain Tax Positions_** 

|_Description of_<br>_the Matter_|As discussed in Notes 1 and 9 of the consolidated financial statements, the Company is subject to income<br>taxes in the U.S. and numerous foreign jurisdictions and during the ordinary course of business, there are<br>many tax positions for which the ultimate tax determination is uncertain. As a result, significant judgment is<br>required in evaluating the Company’s tax positions and determining its provision for income taxes. The<br>Company uses significant judgment in (1) determining whether a tax position’s technical merits are more<br>likely than not to be sustained and (2) measuring the amount of tax benefit that qualifies for recognition. As of<br>December 31, 2025, the Company reported accrued liabilities of $6.6 billion for various tax contingencies.<br>Auditing the recognition and measurement of certain of the Company’s uncertain tax positions was<br>challenging because the evaluation of whether a tax position is more likely than not to be sustained and the<br>measurement of the benefit of various tax positions can be complex and involves significant auditor<br>judgment. Management’s evaluation of tax positions is based on interpretations of tax laws and legal rulings,<br>and may be impacted by regulatory changes and judicial and examination activity.|
|---|---|
|_How We_<br>_Addressed the_<br>_Matter in Our_<br>_Audit_|We tested controls over the Company’s process to assess the technical merits of its tax contingencies,<br>including controls over: the assessment as to whether a tax position is more likely than not to be sustained;<br>the measurement of the benefit of its tax positions, both initially and on an ongoing basis; and the<br>development of the related disclosures.|
||We involved our tax subject matter professionals in assessing the technical merits of certain of the Company’s<br>tax positions. Depending on the nature of the specific tax position and, as applicable, developments with the<br>relevant tax authorities relating thereto, our procedures included obtaining and examining the Company’s<br>analysis including the Company’s correspondence with such tax authorities and evaluating the underlying<br>facts upon which the tax positions are based. We used our knowledge of and experience with income tax laws<br>of the relevant taxing jurisdictions to evaluate the Company’s accounting for its tax contingencies. We<br>evaluated developments in the applicable regulatory environments to assess potential effects on the<br>Company’s positions, including recent decisions in relevant court cases. We analyzed the appropriateness of<br>the Company’s assumptions and the accuracy of the Company’s calculations and data used to determine the<br>amount of tax benefits to recognize. We evaluated the Company’s income tax disclosures in relation to these<br>matters.|
