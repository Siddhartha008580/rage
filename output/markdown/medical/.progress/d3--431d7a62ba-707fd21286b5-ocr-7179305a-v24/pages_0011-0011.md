| What is the most important information I should know about RYBELSUS® and<br>OZEMPIC® tablets?<br>RYBELSUS® and OZEMPIC® tablets may cause serious side effects, including:<br>• Possible thyroid tumors, including cancer. Tell your healthcare provider if you get a lump<br>or swelling in your neck, hoarseness, trouble swallowing, or shortness of breath. These may<br>be symptoms of thyroid cancer. In studies with rodents, RYBELSUS® and OZEMPIC® tablets<br>and other medicines that work like RYBELSUS® and OZEMPIC® tablets caused thyroid tumors,<br>including thyroid cancer. It is not known if RYBELSUS® and OZEMPIC® tablets will cause thyroid<br>tumors or a type of thyroid cancer called medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) in people.<br>• Do not use RYBELSUS® or OZEMPIC® tablets if you or any of your family have ever had a type<br>of thyroid cancer called medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC), or if you have an endocrine system<br>condition called Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2). |  |
| What are RYBELSUS® and OZEMPIC® tablets?<br>RYBELSUS® and OZEMPIC® tablets are prescription medicines used:<br>• along with diet and exercise to improve blood sugar (glucose) in adults with type 2 diabetes<br>mellitus.<br>• to reduce the risk of major cardiovascular events such as heart attack, stroke or death in adults<br>with type 2 diabetes mellitus who are at high risk for these events.<br>It is not known if RYBELSUS® and OZEMPIC® tablets are safe and effective for use in children. |  |

Advice is<br>also available online at poisonhelp.org.<br>Your dose of RYBELSUS® or OZEMPIC® tablets and other diabetes medicines may<br>need to change because of: change in level of physical activity or exercise, weight gain or<br>loss, increased stress, illness, change in diet, fever, trauma, infection, surgery or because of other<br>medicines you take. |  |
