# **Important** 

- Caregivers must **be very careful when handling used needles** to prevent accidental needle stick injuries and prevent passing (transmission) of infection. 

- Never use a syringe to withdraw OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> from your pen. 

- **Always carry an extra pen and new needles** with you, in case of loss or damage. 

- Always keep your pen and needles **out of reach of others** , especially children. 

- **Always keep your pen with you.** Do not leave it in a car or other place where it can get too hot or too cold. 

# **Caring for your pen** 

- **Do not drop your pen** or knock it against hard surfaces. If you drop it or suspect a problem, attach a new needle and check the OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> flow before you inject. 

- **Do not try to repair your pen** or pull it apart. 

- **Do not expose your pen to dust, dirt or liquid.** 

- **Do not wash, soak, or lubricate your pen.** If necessary, clean it with mild detergent on a moistened cloth. 

# **How should I store my OZEMPIC**<sup>**®**</sup> **pen?** 

• Store your **new, unused** OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> pens in the refrigerator between 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C). • **Store your pen in use** for 56 days at room temperature between 59°F to 86°F (15°C to 30°C) or in a refrigerator between 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C). 

• The OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> pen you are using should be disposed of (thrown away) after 56 days, even if it still has OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> left in it. Write the disposal date on your calendar. • **Do not** freeze OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> . **Do not** use OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> if it has been frozen. 

- Unused OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> pens may be used until the expiration date (“EXP”) printed on the label, if kept in the refrigerator. 

- When stored in the refrigerator, **do not** store OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> pens directly next to the cooling element. 

- Keep OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> away from heat and out of the light. 

- **Keep the pen cap on when not in use.** 

- **Keep OZEMPIC**<sup>**®**</sup> **and all medicines out of the reach of children.**
