**OZEMPIC**<sup>**®**</sup> **(semaglutide) injection, for subcutaneous use  Medication Guide** 

| Medication Guide<br>OZEMPIC® (oh-ZEM-pick)<br>(semaglutide) injection, for subcutaneous use | How should I use OZEMPIC®?<br>• Read the Instructions for Use that comes with OZEMPIC®.<br>• Use OZEMPIC® exactly as your healthcare provider tells you to.<br>• Your healthcare provider should show you how to use OZEMPIC® before you use it for the<br>first time.<br>• OZEMPIC® is injected under the skin (subcutaneously) of your stomach (abdomen), thigh, or upper arm.<br>Do not inject OZEMPIC® into a muscle (intramuscularly) or vein (intravenously).<br>• Use OZEMPIC® 1 time each week, on the same day each week, at any time of the day.<br>• You may change the day of the week you use OZEMPIC® as long as your last dose was given 2 or more<br>days before.<br>• If you miss a dose of OZEMPIC®, take the missed dose as soon as possible within 5 days after the missed<br>dose. If more than 5 days have passed, skip the missed dose and take your next dose on the regularly<br>scheduled day.<br>• OZEMPIC® may be taken with or without food.<br>• Do not mix insulin and OZEMPIC® together in the same injection.<br>• You may give an injection of OZEMPIC® and insulin in the same body area (such as your stomach area),<br>but not right next to each other.<br>• Change (rotate) your injection site with each injection. Do not use the same site for each injection.<br>• Do not share your OZEMPIC® pen with other people, even if the needle has been changed.<br>You may give other people a serious infection, or get a serious infection from them.<br>• If you take too much OZEMPIC®, call your healthcare provider or Poison Help line at 1-800-222-1222 or<br>go to the nearest hospital emergency room right away. |

| What is the most important information I should know about OZEMPIC®?<br>OZEMPIC® may cause serious side effects, including:<br>• Possible thyroid tumors, including cancer. Tell your healthcare provider<br>if you get a lump or swelling in your neck, hoarseness, trouble swallowing, or<br>shortness of breath. These may be symptoms of thyroid cancer. In studies with<br>rodents, OZEMPIC® and medicines that work like OZEMPIC® caused thyroid<br>tumors, including thyroid cancer. It is not known if OZEMPIC® will cause thyroid<br>tumors or a type of thyroid cancer called medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) in<br>people.<br>• Do not use OZEMPIC® if you or any of your family have ever had a type of thyroid<br>cancer called medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC), or if you have an endocrine<br>system condition called Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2). |  |
| What is OZEMPIC®?<br>OZEMPIC® is an injectable prescription medicine used:<br>• along with diet and exercise to improve blood sugar (glucose) in adults with type 2<br>diabetes mellitus.<br>• to reduce the risk of major cardiovascular events such as heart attack, stroke or<br>death in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus with known heart disease.<br>• to reduce the risk of kidney disease worsening, kidney failure (end-stage kidney<br>disease), and death due to cardiovascular disease in adults with type 2 diabetes<br>mellitus and chronic kidney disease.<br>It is not known if OZEMPIC® is safe and effective for use in children. | What are the possible side effects of OZEMPIC®?<br>OZEMPIC® may cause serious side effects, including:<br>• See “What is the most important information I should know about OZEMPIC®?”<br>• inflammation of your pancreas (pancreatitis). Stop using OZEMPIC® and call your healthcare<br>provider right away if you have severe pain in your stomach area (abdomen) that will not go away, with or<br>without vomiting. You may feel the pain from your abdomen to your back.<br>• changes in vision. Tell your healthcare provider if you have changes in vision during treatment with<br>OZEMPIC®.<br>• low blood sugar (hypoglycemia). Your risk for getting low blood sugar may be higher if you use<br>OZEMPIC® with another medicine that can cause low blood sugar, such as a sulfonylurea or insulin. Signs<br>and symptoms of low blood sugar may include:<br>• dizziness or light-headedness • blurred vision • anxiety, irritability, or mood changes<br>• sweating • slurred speech • hunger<br>• confusion or drowsiness • shakiness • weakness<br>• headache • fast heartbeat • feeling jittery<br>• Dehydration leading to kidney problems. Diarrhea, nausea, and vomiting may cause a loss of fluids<br>(dehydration) which may cause kidney problems. It is important for you to drink fluids to help reduce your<br>chance of dehydration. Tell your healthcare provider right away if you have nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea<br>that does not go away.<br>• Severe stomach problems. Stomach problems, sometimes severe, have been reported in people who<br>use OZEMPIC®. Tell your healthcare provider if you have stomach problems that are severe or will not go<br>away.<br>• serious allergic reactions. Stop using OZEMPIC® and get medical help right away, if you have any<br>symptoms of a serious allergic reaction including:<br>• swelling of your face, lips, tongue or throat • problems breathing or swallowing<br>• severe rash or itching • fainting or feeling dizzy • very rapid heartbeat<br>• gallbladder problems. Gallbladder problems have happened in some people who take OZEMPIC®.<br>Tell your healthcare provider right away if you get symptoms of gallbladder problems which may include:<br>• pain in your upper stomach (abdomen) • yellowing of skin or eyes (jaundice)<br>• fever • clay-colored stools<br>• food or liquid getting into the lungs during surgery or other procedures that use anesthesia<br>or deep sleepiness (deep sedation). OZEMPIC® may increase the chance of food getting into your<br>lungs during surgery or other procedures. Tell all your healthcare providers that you are taking OZEMPIC®<br>before you are scheduled to have surgery or other procedures.<br>The most common side effects of OZEMPIC® may include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, stomach<br>(abdominal) pain, and constipation.<br>Talk to your healthcare provider about any side effect that bothers you or does not go away.<br>These are not all the possible side effects of OZEMPIC®.<br>Call your doctor for medical advice about side effects. You may report side effects to FDA at<br>1-800-FDA-1088. |
