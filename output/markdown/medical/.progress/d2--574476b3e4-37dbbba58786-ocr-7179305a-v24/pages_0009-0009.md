# _Recommended Storage Conditions for OZEMPIC_ 

<u>Single-patient-use pens (with multiple weekly doses):</u> 

Prior to first use, the OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> pen should be stored in a refrigerator between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Do not store in the freezer or directly adjacent to the refrigerator cooling element. Do not freeze OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> and do not use OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> if it has been frozen. OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> must be kept in the original carton until time of administration. 

After first use of the OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> pen, the pen can be stored for 56 days at controlled room temperature 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) or in a refrigerator 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Do not freeze. Keep the pen cap on when not in use. OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> should be protected from excessive heat and sunlight. 

Always remove and safely discard the needle after each injection and store the OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> pen without an injection needle attached. Always use a new needle for each injection.

# **17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION**

Advise the patient to read the FDA-approved patient labeling (Medication Guide and Instructions for Use). 

## _Risk of Thyroid C-cell Tumors_

Inform patients that semaglutide causes thyroid C-cell tumors in rodents and that the human relevance of this finding has not been determined. Counsel patients to report symptoms of thyroid tumors (e.g., a lump in the neck, hoarseness, dysphagia, or dyspnea) to their physician [see Boxed Warning, Warnings and Precautions (5.1)]. 

## _Acute Pancreatitis_

Inform patients of the potential risk for acute pancreatitis and its symptoms: severe abdominal pain that may radiate to the back, and which may or may not be accompanied by nausea or vomiting. Instruct patients to discontinue OZEMPIC<sup>®</sup> promptly and contact their physician if pancreatitis is suspected [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
