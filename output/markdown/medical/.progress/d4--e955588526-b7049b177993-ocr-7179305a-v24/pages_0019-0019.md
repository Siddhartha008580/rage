**WEGOVY**<sup>**®**</sup> **(semaglutide) injection and WEGOVY**<sup>**®**</sup> **(semaglutide) tablets  Medication Guide** 

| MEDICATION GUIDE<br>WEGOVY® (wee-GOH-vee) WEGOVY® (wee-GOH-vee)<br>(semaglutide) (semaglutide)<br>injection, for subcutaneous use tablets, for oral use | How should I use WEGOVY®?<br>• Use WEGOVY® exactly as your healthcare provider tells you to.<br>• Use WEGOVY® with a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity.<br>• If you take too much WEGOVY®, you may have severe nausea, severe vomiting and severe<br>low blood sugar. Call your healthcare provider or Poison Help line at 1-800-222-1222 or<br>go to the nearest hospital emergency room right away. Advice is also available online at<br>poisonhelp.org.<br>WEGOVY® Injection<br>• Read the Instructions for Use that comes with WEGOVY®.<br>• Your healthcare provider should tell you which WEGOVY® product you will be<br>using (for example – single-dose pen or syringe, single-patient-use pen, or<br>tablets). Before you use WEGOVY® injection for the first time, your healthcare provider<br>should show you how to use it . Once you have been trained, you may inject WEGOVY®<br>yourself if your healthcare provider determines that you are able to do so safely. If you are<br>visually impaired, the WEGOVY® FlexTouch® pen is not recommended for self-injection.<br>• If you are prescribed a different WEGOVY® product at any point, make sure you receive new<br>training for the new product and read the Instructions for Use that come with it.<br>• WEGOVY® is injected under the skin (subcutaneously) of your stomach (abdomen), thigh, or<br>upper arm. Do not inject WEGOVY® into a muscle (intramuscularly) or vein (intravenously).<br>• Change (rotate) your injection site with each injection. Do not use the same site for each<br>injection.<br>• Use WEGOVY® 1 time each week, on the same day each week, at any time of<br>the day.<br>• If you need to change the day of the week, you may do so as long as your last dose of<br>WEGOVY® was given 2 or more days before.<br>• If you miss a dose of WEGOVY® and the next scheduled dose is more than 2 days away (48<br>hours), take the missed dose as soon as possible. If you miss a dose of WEGOVY® and the<br>next scheduled dose is less than 2 days away (48 hours), do not administer the dose. Take<br>your next dose on the regularly scheduled day.<br>• If you miss doses of WEGOVY® for 2 or more consecutive weeks, contact your healthcare<br>provider to talk about how to restart your treatment.<br>• You can take WEGOVY® with or without food.<br>WEGOVY® Tablets<br>• Take 1 WEGOVY® tablet by mouth on an empty stomach in the morning with water (no more<br>than 4 ounces). Do not take WEGOVY® tablets with any other liquids besides water.<br>• Do not split, crush, chew or dissolve WEGOVY® tablets. Swallow WEGOVY® tablets whole.<br>• Do not take more than 1 WEGOVY® tablet each day.<br>• After 30 minutes, you can eat, drink, or take other oral medicines.<br>• If you miss a dose of WEGOVY®, skip the missed dose and go back to your regular schedule<br>for the next dose. |

| What is the most important information I should know about WEGOVY®?<br>WEGOVY® may cause serious side effects, including:<br>• Possible thyroid tumors, including cancer. Tell your healthcare provider if you get<br>a lump or swelling in your neck, hoarseness, trouble swallowing, or shortness of breath.<br>These may be symptoms of thyroid cancer. In studies with rodents, WEGOVY® and other<br>medicines that work like WEGOVY® caused thyroid tumors, including thyroid cancer. It<br>is not known if WEGOVY® will cause thyroid tumors or a type of thyroid cancer called<br>medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) in people.<br>• Do not use WEGOVY® if you or any of your family have ever had a type of thyroid cancer<br>called medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC), or if you have an endocrine system condition<br>called Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2). |  |
| What is WEGOVY®?<br>• WEGOVY® injection is a prescription medicine used with a reduced-calorie diet and<br>increased physical activity to:<br>• reduce the risk of major cardiovascular events such as death, heart attack, or stroke in<br>adults with known heart disease and with either obesity or overweight.<br>• help adults and children aged 12 years and older with obesity, or some adults with<br>excess weight (overweight) who also have weight-related medical problems to lose<br>weight and keep the weight off.<br>• treat adults with metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) with<br>moderate to advanced liver scarring (fibrosis), but not with cirrhosis of the liver.<br>• WEGOVY® tablets are a prescription medicine used with a reduced-calorie diet and<br>increased physical activity to:<br>• reduce the risk of major cardiovascular events such as death, heart attack, or stroke in<br>adults with known heart disease and with either obesity or overweight.<br>• help adults with obesity, or some adults with excess weight (overweight) who also have<br>weight-related medical problems to lose weight and keep the weight off.<br>• WEGOVY® contains semaglutide and should not be used with other semaglutide-containing<br>products or other GLP-1 receptor agonist medicines.<br>• It is not known if WEGOVY® injection is safe and effective:<br>• to reduce the risk of major cardiovascular events (death, heart attack, or stroke) in people<br>under 18 years.<br>• to help children under 12 years of age lose weight and keep the weight off.<br>• for the treatment of MASH in people under 18 years of age.<br>• It is not known if WEGOVY® tablets are safe and effective for use in people under 18 years of<br>age. |  |
|  | What are the possible side effects of WEGOVY®?<br>WEGOVY® may cause serious side effects, including:<br>• See “What is the most important information I should know about WEGOVY®?”<br>• inflammation of your pancreas (pancreatitis). Stop using WEGOVY® and call your<br>healthcare provider right away if you have severe pain in your stomach area (abdomen) that<br>will not go away, with or without nausea or vomiting. Sometimes you may feel the pain from<br>your abdomen to your back.<br>• gallbladder problems. WEGOVY® may cause gallbladder problems including gallstones.<br>Some gallbladder problems need surgery. Call your healthcare provider if you have any of<br>the following symptoms:<br>• pain in your upper stomach (abdomen) • yellowing of skin or eyes (jaundice)<br>• fever • clay-colored stools<br>• increased risk of low blood sugar (hypoglycemia) in patients with type 2<br>diabetes, especially those who also take medicines to treat type 2 diabetes<br>mellitus such as an insulin or a sulfonylureas. Low blood sugar in patients with<br>type 2 diabetes who receive WEGOVY® can be both a serious and common side effect. Talk<br>to your healthcare provider about how to recognize and treat low blood sugar. You should<br>check your blood sugar before you start taking WEGOVY® and while you take WEGOVY®.<br>Signs and symptoms of low blood sugar may include:<br>• dizziness or light-headedness • sweating • shakiness<br>• blurred vision • slurred speech • weakness<br>• anxiety • hunger • headache<br>• irritability or mood changes • confusion or drowsiness • fast heartbeat<br>• feeling jittery<br>• dehydration leading to kidney problems. Diarrhea, nausea, and vomiting may cause<br>a loss of fluids (dehydration) which may cause kidney problems. It is important for you to<br>drink fluids to help reduce your chance of dehydration. Tell your healthcare provider right<br>away if you have nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea that does not go away.<br>• severe stomach problems. Stomach problems, sometimes severe, have been reported<br>in people who use WEGOVY®. Tell your healthcare provider if you have stomach problems<br>that are severe or will not go away.<br>• serious allergic reactions. Stop using WEGOVY® and get medical help right away, if<br>you have any symptoms of a serious allergic reaction including:<br>• swelling of your face, lips, tongue or throat<br>• severe rash or itching • very rapid heartbeat<br>• problems breathing or swallowing • fainting or feeling dizzy<br>• change in vision in people with type 2 diabetes. Tell your healthcare provider if you<br>have changes in vision during treatment with WEGOVY®.<br>• increased heart rate. WEGOVY® can increase your heart rate while you are at rest.<br>Your healthcare provider should check your heart rate while you take WEGOVY®. Tell your<br>healthcare provider if you feel your heart racing or pounding in your chest and it lasts for<br>several minutes. |

| Before using WEGOVY®, tell your healthcare provider if you have any other<br>medical conditions, including if you:<br>• have or have had problems with your pancreas or kidneys.<br>• have type 2 diabetes and a history of diabetic retinopathy.<br>• are scheduled to have surgery or other procedures that use anesthesia or deep sleepiness<br>(deep sedation).<br>• are pregnant or plan to become pregnant. WEGOVY® may harm your unborn baby.<br>You should stop using WEGOVY® 2 months before you plan to become pregnant.<br>• Pregnancy Exposure Registry: There is a pregnancy exposure registry for women<br>who use WEGOVY® during pregnancy. The purpose of this registry is to collect<br>information about the health of you and your baby. Talk to your healthcare provider about<br>how you can take part in this registry or you may contact Novo Nordisk at<br>1-877-390-2760 or www.wegovypregnancyregistry.com.<br>• are breastfeeding or plan to breastfeed. Breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment<br>with WEGOVY® tablets. It is not known if WEGOVY® when received through an injection<br>passes into your breast milk. You should talk with your healthcare provider about the best<br>way to feed your baby while using WEGOVY®.<br>Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take, including<br>prescription and over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and herbal supplements. WEGOVY®<br>may affect the way some medicines work and some medicines may affect the way WEGOVY®<br>works. Tell your healthcare provider if you are taking other medicines to treat diabetes,<br>including sulfonylureas or insulin. WEGOVY® slows stomach emptying and can affect<br>medicines that need to pass through the stomach quickly.<br>Know the medicines you take.
