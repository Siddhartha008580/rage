**Step 3. Set your dose** Turn the dose selector until the **dose counter** I **stops** , and it **shows your 2.4 mg dose** . See **Figure I** . **2.4 mg selected** The dashed line in the dose counter will guide you to J your 2.4 mg dose. See **Figure J** . **0 2.4** 





The dose selector clicks differently when turned forward or backwards. You will hear a ‘click’ every time you turn the dose selector. 





**Do not set the dose by counting the number of clicks you hear.** 

You can only select 2.4 mg for each dose. When your pen contains less than 2.4 mg, the dose counter stops before 2.4 mg is shown. There is not enough medicine for your dose injection. 

**When your prescribed dose of 2.4 mg lines up with the dose pointer, you have selected your dose.** See **Figure I** .
