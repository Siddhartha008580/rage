# **Important information** 

**Read this Instructions for Use before you start using WEGOVY**<sup>**®**</sup> **. This information does not replace talking to your healthcare provider about your medical condition or treatment.** 

# **Do not use your WEGOVY**<sup>**®**</sup> **pen without receiving training** 

**from your healthcare provider.** Make sure that you or your 

caregiver know how to give an injection with the pen before you 

start your treatment. 

- **Your WEGOVY**<sup>**®**</sup> **pen is for 1 time use only.** The WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> pen is for subcutaneous (under the skin) use only. 

Read and follow the instructions so that you use your 

WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> pen correctly: 

- **The dose of WEGOVY**<sup>**®**</sup> **is already set on your pen.** 

# **Preparation** 

- **The needle is covered by the needle cover and the needle will not be seen.** 

# **Step 1. Prepare for your injection.** 

- Do not remove the pen cap until you are ready to inject. 

   - **Supplies you will need to give your WEGOVY**<sup>**®**</sup> **injection:** 

- Do not touch or push on the needle cover. You could get a needle stick injury. 

- Your WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> injection will start when the needle cover is pressed firmly against your skin. 

   - WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> pen 

- **Do not** remove the pen from your skin before the yellow bar in the pen window has stopped moving. The medicine may appear on the skin or squirt from the needle and you may not get your full dose of WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> if: 

- 1 alcohol swab or soap and water

   - **Check your WEGOVY**<sup>**®**</sup> **pen.** 

- If the yellow bar does not start moving or stops during the injection, contact your healthcare provider or Novo Nordisk at startWegovy.com or call Novo Nordisk Inc. at 1-833-934-6891. 

   - Do not use your WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> pen if: 

   - The pen appears to have been used or any part of the pen appears broken, for example if it has been dropped. 

- The needle cover will lock when the pen is removed from your skin. **You cannot stop the injection and restart it later.** 

   - The WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> medicine is not clear and colorless through the pen window. 

- People who are blind or have vision problems should not use the WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> pen without help from a person trained to use the WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> pen. 

- The expiration date (EXP) has passed. 

# **How do I store WEGOVY**<sup>**®**</sup> **?** 

- Store the WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> pen in the refrigerator between 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C). 

Contact Novo Nordisk at 1-833-934-6891 if your WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> pen fails any of these checks. 

- If needed, before removing the pen cap, WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> can be stored from 46°F to 86°F (8°C to 30°C) in the original carton for up to 28 days. 

# **Step 2. Choose your injection site.** 

- Do not store WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> in the freezer or directly beside the refrigerator cooling element. 

- Your healthcare provider can help you choose the injection site that is best for you. 





- **Do not freeze WEGOVY**<sup>**®**</sup> **and do not use if it has been frozen.** 

- Keep WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> in the original carton to protect it from light. 

- You may inject into your upper leg (front of the thighs), lower stomach (keep 2 inches away from your belly button) or upper arm.
