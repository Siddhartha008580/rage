WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> (semaglutide) injection and WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> (semaglutide) tablets 

_Administration_ Instruct patients how to prepare and administer the correct dose of WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> and assess their ability to inject subcutaneously to ensure the proper administration of WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> . Inform patients and their caregiver(s) which WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> presentation (e.g., single-dose pen or syringe, WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> FlexTouch<sup>®</sup> single-patient-use pen) they will receive and ensure they receive training appropriate for that specific presentation. If the prescribed WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> presentation changes, ensure patients and caregivers receive appropriate training and instruct them to consult the Instructions for Use for the newly prescribed presentation. Advise caregivers that WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> FlexTouch<sup>®</sup> is not recommended for self-administration by those with visual impairment unless their healthcare provider determines the patient can properly administer [see Dosage and Administration (2.1)]. _Missed Doses_ 

Inform patients if a dose of WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> injection is missed and the next scheduled dose is more than 2 days away (48 hours), administer WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> injection as soon as possible. If one dose is missed and the next scheduled dose is less than 2 days away (48 hours), do not administer the dose. Inform patients to resume their regular once-weekly dosing schedule [see Dosage and Administration (2.4)]. Inform patients if a dose of WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> tablets is missed, skip the missed dose and take the next dose the following day [see Dosage and Administration (2.4)]. 

_Lactation_ Advise females not to breastfeed during treatment with WEGOVY<sup>®</sup> tablets [see Use in Specific Populations (8.2)].
