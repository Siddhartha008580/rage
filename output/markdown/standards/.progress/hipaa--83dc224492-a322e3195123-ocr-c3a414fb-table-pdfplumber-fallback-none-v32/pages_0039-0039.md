**§ 162.404   Compliance dates of the implementation of the standard unique health identifier for health care providers.** 

(a) _Health care providers._ A covered health care provider must comply with the implementation specifications in § 162.410 no later than May 23, 2007. 

(b) _Health plans._ A health plan must comply with the implementation specifications in § 162.412 no later than one of the following dates: 

(1) A health plan that is not a small health plan—May 23, 2007. 

(2) A small health plan—May 23, 2008. 

(c) _Health care clearinghouses._ A health care clearinghouse 

must comply with the implementation specifications in § 162.414 no later than May 23, 2007.

# **§ 162.406   Standard unique health identifier for health care providers.** 

(a) _Standard._ The standard unique health identifier for health care providers is the National Provider Identifier (NPI). The NPI is a 10-position numeric identifier, with a check digit in the 10th position, and no intelligence about the health care provider in the number. 

(b) _Required and permitted uses for the NPI._ (1) The NPI must be used as stated in § 162.410, § 162.412, and § 162.414. 

(2) The NPI may be used for any other lawful purpose.
