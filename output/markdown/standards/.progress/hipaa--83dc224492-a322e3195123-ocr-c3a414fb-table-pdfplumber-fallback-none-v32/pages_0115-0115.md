# **§ 164.534   Compliance dates for initial implementation of the privacy standards.** 

(a) _Health care providers._ A covered health care provider must comply with the applicable requirements of this subpart no later than April 14, 2003. 

(b) _Health plans._ A health plan must comply with the applicable requirements of this subpart no later than the following as applicable: 

(1) _Health plans other han small health plans._ April 14, 2003. 

(2) _Small health plans._ April 14, 2004. 

(c) _Health clearinghouses._ A health care clearinghouse must comply with the applicable requirements of this subpart no later than April 14, 2003.
