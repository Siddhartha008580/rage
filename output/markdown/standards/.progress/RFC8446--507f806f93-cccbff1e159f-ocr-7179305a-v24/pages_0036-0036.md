These values refer solely to signatures which appear in certificates (see` <u>`Section 4.4.2.2) and`</u> `are not defined for use in signed TLS handshake messages, although they MAY appear in "signature_algorithms" and "signature_algorithms_cert" for backward compatibility with TLS 1.2. Clients offering these values MUST list them as the lowest priority (listed after all other algorithms in SignatureSchemeList). TLS 1.3 servers MUST NOT offer a SHA-1 signed certificate unless no valid certificate chain can be produced without it (see` <u>`Section 4.4.2.2).`</u> 

The signatures on certificates that are self-signed or certificates that are trust anchors are not validated, since they begin a certification path (see[RFC5280], Section 3.2).  A certificate that begins a certification path MAY use a signature algorithm that is not advertised as being supported in the "signature_algorithms" extension.
